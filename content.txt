--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eyebrow:  Process

H2:
  Five weeks, start to live.

  01 — Discovery — Week 1
    We work out how leads actually reach you today, where they go cold, and what
    a booked meeting is worth. That decides everything we build.

  02 — Build — Weeks 2–4
    Site and agent are built in parallel. You see working software as it lands,

    sharper as we learn what your buyers ask.

================================================================================
NOTES WORTH KNOWING BEFORE EDITING
================================================================================
1. The email address appears in three places that do NOT stay in sync: the
   footer (hard-coded), lib/site.ts (used in structured data), and the contact
   section (admin-editable, from the database).

2. Two success messages on the site — in the callback dialog and the inline
   demo — change wording depending on whether the call was actually placed. With
   HERMES_WEBHOOK_URL unset, no call is dispatched and the honest "we have your
   number" version is shown instead of "your phone is about to ring".

3. Numbers used as claims: "five minutes", "nine minutes from form to booked
   meeting", "five weeks start to live", and the timeline steps. The only
   third-party statistics on the site are inside the research card in the Speed
   to Lead section, and the source file records that they were transcribed from
   artwork rather than verified against the papers.

4. No client counts, conversion percentages or revenue figures are claimed
   anywhere. The Outcomes section is deliberately written to avoid them.
